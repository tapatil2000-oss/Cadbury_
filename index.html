The Gap In Everyday Gifting

Why do we wait for calendar dates to express gratitude or joy?

01

Target Demographics

18–30-year-olds, students, and young professionals in metros and Tier-2 cities who interact daily through quick micro-moments.

02

The Awkward Gap

We feel small thank-yous and sorrys every day, but lack an easy, non-awkward mechanism to voice them out loud.

03

Big vs. Small

Big gifts feel like too much commitment. A simple WhatsApp text feels too cheap or lazy.

04

Dairy Milk Solution
